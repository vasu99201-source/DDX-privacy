1. About the App

DDXOTP provides temporary secondary phone numbers so you can keep your personal number safe. You can view the text messages (SMS) that arrive on these numbers, detect verification codes, copy them with one tap, and get a notification when a new message arrives on a number you have saved. The App does not read the SMS messages stored on your own phone.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Username: the display name you enter during onboarding.
  • Saved items: numbers you assign or bookmark, and messages you choose to save.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and notification identifiers: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token so we can deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Diagnostics and usage data: crash reports, performance data and basic App usage events, if enabled (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics).
  • Server logs: IP address, request time and App version, which our servers record when the App connects.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your personal phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your personal SMS inbox.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the App: show numbers and messages, detect codes, send notifications.
  • To sign you in and keep your saved items.
  • To diagnose crashes, improve performance and develop new features.
  • To prevent abuse, spam and fraud, and to enforce our rules.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed for those services:

  • Google / Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Koyeb, the cloud platform that hosts our backend servers.
  • Authorities, if we are required to by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

Messages received on a secondary number may be visible to other users of the App (see the next section).

5. Using secondary numbers safely

Use a secondary number to keep your personal number safe. Numbers in DDXOTP are temporary and can be used by more than one person at a time, so other users may be able to see messages received on the same number, including verification codes.

  • Use them for secondary or low-risk sign-ups. Do not use them for banking, payments, your primary email, or any account you care about.
  • Do not use the App for fraud, harassment, spam, or to break the rules of any other service.
  • Do not send personal or confidential information to these numbers.

We are not responsible for accounts created with secondary numbers, or for messages seen by other users. Third-party names such as Telegram, WhatsApp or Google mentioned in messages belong to their respective owners; we are not affiliated with them.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet: to load numbers and messages.
  • Notifications: to alert you about new codes and let you copy them from the notification.
  • Foreground service: to keep a connection open and deliver alerts quickly while you are monitoring numbers. A persistent notification is shown while it runs.
  • Run at startup: to resume monitoring of your saved numbers after your phone restarts.

The App does not request SMS or call-log permissions.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Messages received on secondary numbers are archived or removed from our servers periodically.
  • Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Account data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from the App settings, or by emailing us at the address below. We will process requests within 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period for security purposes and then deleted.

8. Security

All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS encryption). We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Sign out at any time, or use the App without saving numbers.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Opt out of analytics where the App provides the option.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or Indian data protection law), you may have additional rights.
